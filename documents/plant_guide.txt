Plant Disease Guide

1. Early Blight of Tomato
Early blight is a fungal disease that can affect tomato leaves.
Symptoms may include brown spots, often with concentric rings.
Remove severely affected plant material and avoid wetting leaves during irrigation.

2. Late Blight of Tomato
Late blight can spread rapidly in suitable environmental conditions.
Symptoms can include dark, water-soaked lesions on leaves.
Seek expert advice to confirm the disease and select suitable control measures.

3. Healthy Tomato Plants
Healthy tomato leaves generally have an even green color.
Inspect plants regularly and provide appropriate watering, nutrition, and air circulation.

Important:
These notes are educational and are not a substitute for professional plant diagnosis.

Plant Leaf Health Guide

Potato early blight may appear as brown leaf spots, sometimes with concentric rings.
Potato late blight may cause dark, water-soaked-looking lesions on leaves and can spread rapidly in suitable conditions.
Similar symptoms can have different causes. A photo alone may not be enough to identify a plant disease reliably.

Inspect several leaves and compare symptoms with trusted local agricultural guidance.
These notes are educational and are not a substitute for professional plant diagnosis.
